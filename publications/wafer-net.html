Wafer-Net: A Flexible Hierarchical Interconnection Architecture Based on Wafer-Scale Integration

Zhenghao Wang, Qiang Wang, Mingche Lai, Jiaqing Xu, Jinbo Xu

College of Computer Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology, Changsha, China

SC26: International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis, 2026, pp. 2117–2136

[DOI]

Abstract

High-performance interconnection networks are typically built upon high-radix routers. As system scale continues to grow, the conventional switch-centric scaling paradigm faces severe challenges in latency, deployment cost, and scalability. This paper proposes Wafer-Net, a flexible hierarchical interconnection architecture enabled by wafer-scale integration. Wafer-Net abstracts distributed on-wafer compute and switch resources into two composable building blocks, the Compute Chiplet Group (CCG) and the Switch Chiplet Group (SCG), and thereby recasts conventional interconnection networks as a hierarchical structure that combines local high-bandwidth domains with global low-latency switching. We further propose a formal mapping framework based on hierarchical compression transformations and dynamic programming. Using this framework, we instantiate three representative Wafer-Net topologies, which significantly reduce network diameter and cabling overhead while supporting concise and efficient minimal/non-minimal and adaptive routing algorithms. Simulation results show that these Wafer-Net topologies consistently outperform both their conventional counterparts and the wafer-scale baseline in throughput and latency.

Index Terms: interconnection network, topology, routing algorithm, wafer-scale integration, chiplet.
